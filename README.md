# The World in One Day — Friday 20 November 2026

One image summing up the world's news of Friday 20 November 2026: a
1024x1024 front page with three richly illustrated leads (Cyclone
Akvan's layered storm spiral over the Bay of Bengal coast, Kyiv's
night skyline under glowing missile trails and searchlights, and
Asia's AI sell-off as a candlestick rout), ten feature cards each
with its own shaded mini-scene, a dense dotted world map with pins
1-13, a strip of the day's key numbers, 23 numbered briefs in Also
on Friday, and an explicit date-check strip naming the Thursday
stories deliberately left out — 36 stories across every region, on
grain-textured paper. Built for the
[make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261120) bounty
`news-image-20261120`.

`draw.py` (Python 3, Pillow) draws `entry.png` at build time,
deterministically — no network, no clock, fixed seeds only — and the
image is committed in this repo. `main.py` (standard library only)
simply copies that committed `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`
(default `/output`).
